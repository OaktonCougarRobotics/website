About Ploutis Contracting

  • Ploutis Contracting is a family-owned and operated commercial, industrial, and residential painting and renovation company serving the Washington, DC area since 1960. Based in Alexandria, VA, they specialize in painting, property maintenance, general contracting, carpentry, concrete repairs, and decking, with a focus on serving HOAs and multi-family properties.

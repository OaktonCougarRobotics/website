About NearStar

  • NearStar Fusion is a company based in Virginia developing a compact Magnetized Target Impact Fusion technology to produce clean, always-on base-load electricity. They use pulsed plasma railguns to create fusion, aiming for a simpler, cheaper, and faster-to-build reactor that avoids the complex magnets and high costs of traditional fusion approaches.
